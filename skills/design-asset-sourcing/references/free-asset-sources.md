# Free-asset source catalog

| Source | What | License | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| free3dicons.com | 3D icons (food, objects) | Free, no attribution, no account | CDN: `cdn.free3dicons.com/icons/<slug>/master.webp` |
| poly.pizza | 3D models | CC0/CC-BY mix | check per-model |
| Sketchfab | 3D models | filter by downloadable + CC | many CC0 |
| IconScout free tier | 3D icons, PNG/glTF | free subset | some need account |

## Reading paid listings (e.g. craftwork.design)

When the user sends a paid product page, extract what the pack contains (icon count, format, resolution) from the listing text, then match it against the free catalog above rather than searching blind. Report the match quality honestly (e.g. "10 icons vs their 50").
